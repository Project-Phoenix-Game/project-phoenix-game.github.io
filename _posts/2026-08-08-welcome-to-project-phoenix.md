Strap in, this is going to be a long journey!

Project Phoenix stems from hours and hours of conversations we've had about the various games in the genre we've played – discussing what we liked, what we disliked, and how we'd fix them if they were in our hands. Eventually, we realized that we had designed our own game in our heads. This is simultaneously the easiest and the hardest part of making a video game – good ideas! We feel we have a solid foundation, the right mix of skills, and the motivation to actually build it.

Project Phoenix is a working title, one with many flaws. For one thing, I can't write "phoenix" properly half the time. But more problematic is the fact that there is already an existing game and an aborted project with that same name. It started as a joke, inspiring us with many lore ideas, and we started calling it that among ourselves before doing a basic internet search. The deed was done.

## What is Project Phoenix?

Project Phoenix will be a 4X massively multiplayer real-time strategy game set in a space opera universe. In practice, that means players explore new regions of space, expand their presence, gather and manage resources, and sometimes go to war – all alongside many other players. You don't play in isolation: every decision you make affects others, and their actions, in turn, affect you.

This all takes place thousands of years in the future, after humanity has left Earth and spread across space aboard countless stations. Most barely manage to survive, drifting through known space in search of resources. Only a few thrive – and even fewer dare to venture into the unknown in pursuit of riches and glory.

At its core, the game is player-driven and alliance-focused, with persistent long-term progression. Players take part in "expeditions" – temporary instanced worlds with their own progression and rules – that serve as focused spaces for competition, cooperation, and strategic play. The outcomes of these expeditions feed back into the persistent universe, allowing players to shape its future, grow their colonies and set out on ever more challenging expeditions.

## Who are we?

We're a two-person team building Project Phoenix, both software engineers with complementary backgrounds. Victor specializes in back-end development and DevOps, focusing on the technical foundations and stability of the game. I come from a game development background, having worked in the gaming industry on player-facing systems and gameplay. Together, we cover both the creative and technical sides of the project.

For now, we're not looking for additional help – we want to get the foundations right and reach a clearer vision shaped before bringing anyone else in. As the project progresses, that will likely change, and we'll be explicit when we're ready to open up specific areas.

## What is the timeline?

Project Phoenix is a serious project for us, but it's something we're building alongside our day-to-day work. That means development will be steady, but not always fast – and our communication won't always be perfectly regular.

Right now, the plan is:

1. Lay down our core concepts / gameplay ideas
2. Build a basic working version (vertical slice) before tackling complex systems.
3. Iterate toward a first playable alpha with the core systems in place from feedback & experience from vertical slice.
4. Expand content and features, then move toward a broader beta.

**We'll share progress when there's something concrete to show, and sometimes that will mean quiet periods while we focus on building.**

## What to expect on the blog?

A project such as Project Phoenix is a massive undertaking. It will take us at least a year until we have a basic working version, and it will be a couple more years to reach a beta version. With this blog, we hope to achieve the following:

1. Dive into the game's design and systems, and explain the reasoning behind our choices.
2. Document our progress, both so you can follow along and to maintain a clear development history.
3. Show what goes on behind the scenes, the problems we run into, and how we work through them.
4. Write and post lore pieces, when the time comes.
5. Keep you interested, and keep us motivated!

**Welcome, and wish us luck!**
